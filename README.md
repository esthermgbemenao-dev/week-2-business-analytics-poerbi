## Week 3 – Advanced Data Analysis

Week 3 builds on the Week 2 dashboard with advanced analysis using Power BI and DAX.

### Key Analysis Areas

- KPI development
- DAX measures and calculated metrics
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Product category and sub-category performance
- Regional and state-level analysis
- Profitability analysis
- Discount and profit analysis
- Business insights and recommendations

### Key Findings

- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286.40K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Total Customers: 793
- Average Sales per Order: $458.61
- California recorded the highest sales at $457,687.63.

### Business Insights

The analysis showed that high sales do not always translate into high profitability. Differences in profitability across categories and sub-categories highlight the importance of evaluating both revenue and profit when making business decisions.

### Recommendations

- Review low-profit and loss-making products.
- Monitor discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Focus on high-performing categories and markets.
- Regularly monitor sales and profitability trends using the dashboard.
